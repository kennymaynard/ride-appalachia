# Appalachia Offroad Beta Launch Packet

## Rider Invite Message

Hey, I am opening a small beta for Appalachia Offroad, a trip-planning app for ATV, UTV, Jeep, and off-road riders in Appalachia.

Can you test it like you are planning a real ride? Try finding a trail, saving a trip, checking nearby lodging/food/fuel/repairs, leaving a review, and using the rider dashboard.

I am looking for honest feedback: what was confusing, what felt useful, what was missing, and anything that broke.

Beta link: https://appalachiaoffroadapp.com

## Business Signup Pitch

Appalachia Offroad helps riders plan the whole trip: trails, lodging, food, fuel, rentals, repairs, deals, and offline planning.

I am inviting a small group of local businesses to join early before the wider rider launch. Early partners can get listed in the marketplace, show up near ride areas, add deals, receive booking/service interest, and be part of the first local rider network.

Best fit: cabins, campgrounds, restaurants, fuel stops, repair shops, rentals, recovery, outfitters, and veteran-owned businesses.

Signup link: https://appalachiaoffroadapp.com/business/join

## 5-10 Person Testing Checklist

- Open the homepage on mobile and desktop.
- Explain what you think the app does after five seconds.
- Search for a ride area or town.
- Open a ride area and scan the trail cards.
- Open the marketplace and filter by lodging, food, fuel, repairs, rentals, Hero Verified, and Veteran Owned.
- Save or download a trip/trail pack.
- Create or open a rider login.
- Mark a trail/activity complete and check badge progress.
- Leave a trail review.
- Switch between rider-facing pages and business-facing pages.
- Try business login or business signup if relevant.
- Report anything broken, confusing, slow, or visually awkward.

## Live Smoke Test

- Rider login works.
- Trip save/download works.
- Badge progress updates after completing an activity.
- Trail review submits and appears in admin/moderation flow.
- Business login works from email/access token.
- Business dashboard shows profile, bookings, service requests, partner deals, and setup status.
- Stripe test checkout works.
- Stripe Connect onboarding opens and returns correctly.
- Privacy, terms, contact, refunds, bookings, and marketplace pages are live.
- Mobile homepage, marketplace, ride areas, planner, rider dashboard, and business dashboard do not overlap or hide controls.
